Welcome to EduChroma Analyticals

EduChroma Analyticals is a leading analytical training and testing centre dedicated to developing industry-ready professionals and providing reliable analytical solutions to industries, researchers, and institutions.

We bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world laboratory experience by offering hands-on training using modern analytical instruments and industry-standard practices.

Specializing in advanced High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) method development, System Suitability Testing (SST), and forced degradation studies following International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) and Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP) standards.

We also provide dedicated academic research guidance, thesis drafting support, and dissertation preparation management for Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) and M.Sc. research scholars at our Vadodara facility.
