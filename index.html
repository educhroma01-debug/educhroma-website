Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About EduChroma Analyticals

Explore our most frequently asked questions about practical HPLC training, analytical services, internship programmes, career guidance and student support.

Training & Learning Courses, instruments and certificates
01What makes EduChroma Analyticals different from other HPLC training centres?
EduChroma focuses on practical, instrument-oriented learning. Students gain hands-on exposure to HPLC operation, UV-Visible Spectroscopy, sample preparation, chromatographic analysis, laboratory documentation and troubleshooting.
02Which instruments and analytical techniques can I learn?
Our training programmes include practical exposure to HPLC, UV-Visible Spectrophotometry, chromatographic data interpretation, sample preparation, analytical method development and laboratory documentation, depending on the selected programme.
03Do you provide online training or only offline laboratory sessions?
Our primary focus is offline, hands-on laboratory training at our Vadodara centre. This allows participants to work directly with analytical instruments and understand real laboratory operations.
04Who can enrol in EduChroma's training programmes?
B.Pharm, M.Pharm, B.Sc, M.Sc Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Life Sciences and related science students, researchers and working professionals can enquire about suitable programmes.
05Will I receive a certificate after completing my training?
Yes. Participants who successfully complete the programme requirements receive an EduChroma training certificate describing their completed training programme.
Career & Student Support Internships, guidance and enrolment
06Does EduChroma provide placement assistance?
We provide career guidance and support for students preparing for opportunities in pharmaceutical, chemical, analytical testing and related industries. Placement or job selection is not guaranteed.
07How does practical HPLC training help fresh graduates?
Practical training helps fresh graduates understand instrument operation, chromatograms, retention time, peak area, sample preparation, basic troubleshooting and analytical laboratory workflows. These skills can support preparation for entry-level QC and analytical roles.
08What is the duration and fee structure of your programmes?
We offer different programme formats, including one-day workshops, one-week practical training, 15-day programmes, one-month internships and advanced specialized training. Fees depend on the programme, duration and training scope. Contact our team for current details.
09Can I join while studying in college or working?
Students and working professionals can enquire about upcoming batch schedules. We can guide you towards an appropriate programme based on your availability and learning requirements.
10How can I register for a training programme?
You can contact EduChroma through WhatsApp or submit an enquiry using our online registration form. Our team will share the available batches, programme details and registration process.
